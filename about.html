About Innovative PNG Ltd

A local Mount Hagen business focused on practical products and reliable service.

Serving Mount Hagen and the Highlands

Innovative PNG Ltd is a local supplier of stationery, mobile phone accessories and laptop essentials based in Mount Hagen, Western Highlands Province.

We serve schools, offices, small businesses and individual customers who need quality everyday products at fair prices — from exercise books and pens to screen protectors, chargers and basic laptop accessories.

When you visit our shop at Tininga Central, you get straightforward advice and help finding what you need.

What we offer

  • Stationery for schools and offices
  • Mobile phone accessories and protection
  • Charging solutions and power accessories
  • Laptop bags, mice and everyday essentials
  • Support for bulk and institutional orders
